# Image-generation prompts

These three direction-only references were generated with Codex's built-in
image-generation tool. They are not runtime textures or sprite atlases.

## Gameplay target

Input references: the pre-pass Pixiball day and night gameplay captures.

```text
Reimagine the supplied Pixiball gameplay screenshot as a cohesive premium
high-density pixel-art game scene. Preserve the readable behind-the-pitcher
baseball composition, catcher, umpire, batter, pitcher, infield, strike-zone
reticle, and broadcast HUD hierarchy, but substantially elevate the craft and
cohesion. Use original high-density cinematic pixel art with deliberate 2-4
pixel clusters, crisp stepped silhouettes, subtly chunky stylized anatomy,
hand-authored material breakup, and painterly pixel lighting. Create expressive
athletic proportions, detailed uniform seams and piping, fabric folds, cleats,
glove stitching, and readable hands. Build a richly authored harbor ballpark
with layered grass, dirt, chalk, brick, painted steel, seating, and atmospheric
skyline depth. Use warm golden-hour key light, cool ambient fill, soft contact
shadows, restrained rim light and bloom, and a shared navy, teal, coral, gold,
cream, and restrained-green palette. Keep the supplied 16:9 gameplay camera and
HUD safe zones. Avoid real brands, copied logos, photorealism, smooth vector
art, voxel styling, blurry upscale, excessive bloom, and noisy facial dither.
```
